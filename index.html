Welcome to Double Dutch Bakery.

We are a community bakery cafe created in partnership with Rumble Coffee Roasters. We bake fresh every morning and source our ingredients locally. Our coffee is roasted a street away, and our goal is to bring the best baked goods that Melbourne has to offer, to the inner-west.

The Story

How Double Dutch Bakery came to be

From a question to a community icon

Double Dutch Bakery began with the team at Rumble Coffee asking the question: 'What if we opened a bakery?' The dreaming started (as did the pastry tasting) and pretty soon, this harebrained scheme brought forth Melbourne's most exciting new bakery.

Double Dutch Bakery was born.

Double Dutch Bakery exists not only to be one of Melbourne's best bakeries but also to create a warm, welcoming and down-to-earth haven where the Kensington community can gather, and taste buds rejoice in the delicious harmony of fresh-baked bread, delicious sandwiches, and the best specialty coffee that Melbourne has to offer.

Coffee

Specialty coffee from Rumble Coffee Roasters

Our coffee philosophy

Double Dutch Bakery is a partnership with Melbourne coffee roastery, Rumble Coffee Roasters. All our coffee is roasted by Rumble, and their commitment to roasting the finest specialty coffee means that Double Dutch is not only your bakery destination, it's also one of the best places to experience the most delicious coffee in Melbourne's coffee scene.

Our coffee team is headed up by Luke, who has worked in and managed some of the best coffee shops across the globe, as well as having roasted for Rumble Coffee Roasters. Luke's commitment to making Double Dutch Bakery the go-to place for Melbourne's best coffee is unparalleled.

House Blend & Single Origins

We use Rumble Coffee's Haymaker blend as our house blend, as well as their range of rotating single origins for espresso and filter coffee.

Take Home Beans

We stock a large selection of Rumble’s coffee to take home. To save on packaging, bring your own container and we’ll charge you by the kilo. You even get a 10 percent discount if you BYO container.
